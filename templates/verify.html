Verify Digital Certificate

Authenticate institutional credentials using unique identity tokens or document hash verification

The unique token is printed at the bottom of the digital certificate and embedded within its QR code.
The system will calculate the SHA-256 cryptographic digest of the uploaded file and compare it with the immutable database record.
How Cryptographic Verification Works:
1. Identity Token: Every certificate receives a globally unique UUIDv4 token during issuance.
2. SHA-256 Hashing: The document content produces a 256-bit cryptographic signature stored in the database.
3. Tamper Detection: If even 1 single byte of the certificate is altered, the SHA-256 digest changes completely, flagging the document as TAMPERED.
